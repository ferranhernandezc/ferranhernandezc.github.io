I am Ferran Hernandez Caralt, a PhD Candidate at the University of Cambridge, supervised by Prof. [Pietro Liò](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/~pl219/). With a background in mathematics and experience in the fundamentals of machine learning, my research focuses on the foundations and limitations of graph neural networks and their higher-order variants, specifically when applied to graph-structured data.

My academic journey started with the [CFIS](https://cfis.upc.edu/en) scholarship, which allowed me to pursue a double degree in Mathematics and Data Science & Engineering at the [Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya](https://www.upc.edu/en). I developed my [Bachelor's thesis](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v251/hernandez-caralt24a.html) at the University of Cambridge under the supervision of Prof. Pietro Liò on Sheaf Neural Networks.

Afterwards, I spent time in industry gaining hands-on R&D and engineering experience at [Qualcomm](https://www.qualcomm.com/) as an AI Research Engineering intern and then at [Axiomatic AI](https://axiomatic-ai.com/) as an AI engineer building agentic systems for photonic integrated circuit design.

Supported by a [La Caixa Postgraduate Fellowship Abroad](https://lacaixafoundation.org/en/postgraduate-fellowships-abroad), I returned to Cambridge for my PhD.
